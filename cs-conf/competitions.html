How competitions lead to papers

Three practical publication routes

Shared-task system papers: SemEval and some NLP workshops invite participating teams to describe reproducible systems. Confirm the exact track, submission policy, page limit and whether accepted papers appear in ACL Anthology. The metric rank and paper peer review are not equivalent.

Competition with separate conference submission: Use PAIEC/NeurIPS, ICASSP and other organizer calls to find research directions, then confirm team registration, reproducibility, OpenReview proceedings or report requirements. There may be a competition paper track with separate decisions.

Winner invitation: Some competitions offer an invitation to submit a two-page paper, oral presentation or a journal article. An invitation is conditional and is not acceptance without review.

Unconfirmed publication: Many leaderboards have no guaranteed archival path. Check the organizer CFP.

Discovery and monthly review

Compare ML Contests, CoDaBench, Kaggle competitions, SemEval 2027 official site, NeurIPS competition tracks and ICASSP Grand Challenges, then validate deadlines on each organizer’s site. Every 30 days, re-evaluate feasibility, compute budget, leaderboard risk, paper requirement, registration gate and novelty.
