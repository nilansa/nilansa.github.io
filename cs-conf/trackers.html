How updates work

Live upstream: Every external link opens the maintainer's current page. Changes there are available as soon as their own site is updated; we do not duplicate, scrape or cache its listings on this directory.

Local curated planner: The CS Conf submission table is a separate checked source snapshot, not an automatically synchronized copy of Hugging Face. Its audit timestamp is displayed on that page.

Authority: For abstract registration, author requirements, proceedings status, AoE timezones and extensions, the official conference or challenge CFP takes priority. Unannounced dates remain unknown.
