# Industry / applied paper tracks — student-author eligibility audit

**Audited 27 October 2026 (IST)** · CS Conf task `20261026-csconf-research` · [Main index](index.html) · [Machine-readable evidence](data.json)

## The question the tracker must answer

**A student does not need an industry job to submit an Industry Track paper when that venue's official call permits academic authors.** But the manuscript must meet the **work-specific** standards (real-world users, deployment, operational constraints, field data, or a suitable research alternative). Conversely, an apparently appropriate industry paper can still be **ineligible** when the venue mandates a non-academic coauthor.

The labels in this audit apply to **a university-only student author team**:
- **✓ Explicit/open**: a 2027 or historical organizer FAQ explicitly permits student/academic authors, or the applied/research call imposes no corporate-author restriction. This is **author eligibility only**, not acceptance or paper readiness. The details identify whether eligibility was explicit or inferred from an open call.
- **✕ Affiliation rule**: at least one non-academic author, or an industry first/majority author, is *mandatory*. Simply conducting an industrial-style experiment at university cannot waive that rule.
- **? Conditional**: the call emphasizes an industrial environment, product users, or company presenter details without clearly confirming academic-only submissions. Ask the chairs; do not mark "student eligible" from silence.
- **History**: a 2026 CFP does not determine 2027 rules. **TBA is not an estimated deadline**. An Industry Day talk is not automatically a proceedings paper. CORE/CCF parent-conference ranking does not separately grade the Industry Track.

### MLSys 2027 — separate industrial and research tracks

The [official MLSys 2027 research and industrial CFP](https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2027/CallForResearchPapers) places Industrial Track eligibility directly in the same page as the research track. It **requires the first and majority of authors to work in industry**, excludes speculative small experiments, and requires real deployed/roadmap/canceled large-scale ML systems with lessons; new algorithmic novelty is *not* mandatory. The deadline for both tracks is **30 October 2026, 20:00 UTC (noon PDT)**; after submitting, authors cannot switch tracks. All papers require detailed **AI Usage Disclosure**. The **research** track remains the route for academically authored original ML-systems research, but it still needs credible evidence; same institutional permissions cannot be assumed for Industrial Track. No CS Conf submission is ready. Source: https://mlsys.org/Conferences/2027/CallForResearchPapers

## NAACL 2027 — detailed decision

**Official call:** https://2027.naacl.org/calls/industry_track/

- **Students and academics are explicitly allowed** by the organizer's FAQ. No company affiliation or proprietary data is required.
- **Deadline:** 31 October 2026, 23:59 AoE. Direct OpenReview submission, **not** ACL Rolling Review. **6 pages** (specified references/limitations/ethics/appendix exclusions); mandatory **Limitations** section.
- **Fit:** NLP/speech applications already deployed or plausibly intended for real-world operation *outside laboratory-only, classroom-only, or experimental-crowdsourcing conditions*; meaningful case studies, real constraints, cost/latency, operational monitoring, negative results, deployment-based vision and reproducible evaluation. An academic benchmark alone is not sufficient.
- **Review:** double-blind, anonymized links. Accepted industry papers are archival in a **separate proceedings volume**; the difference from the research track is topic and evidence focus, not that publication is unofficial.
- **AI / ethics:** paper-writing assistance is permitted under ACL rules; wholly AI-generated papers, fabricated sources, thin-sliced contributions and substantial overlapping submissions can be rejected/sanctioned. Authors must have made substantial contributions and remain accountable.
- **Duplicate submissions:** do not submit the same or substantially overlapping research to NAACL main and Industry Tracks; the CFP specifies review-period and overlap limits. Present/registered author required upon acceptance.
- **Current research-program status:** no existing CS Conf branch has a validated, external-use NLP/speech study ready merely because the author-eligibility rule permits student submissions. Continue to seek evidence, not deadline-driven placeholders.

## Useful applied-research routes for a university team

- **PAKDD 2027 Applied Data Science, 20 Nov 2026 AoE:** no industry coauthor mandate; field pilots and rigorous **realistic lab** evaluations are eligible; methodological novelty is optional but transferable applied insight is mandatory. Crucial restriction: organizer's [author kit](https://www.pakdd2027.org/pages/calls/authors-kit) only permits generative-AI use for grammatical correction, with disclosure.
- **COLING 2027 Industry Track, 30 Nov 2026 AoE:** students explicitly permitted; practical NLP/speech application and evidence still required.
- **VLDB 2027 PVLDB Scalable Data Science research category:** *not* the Industrial Track. Explicitly welcomes educational/scientific/nonprofit/open-source systems and even uses general-purpose **entity linkage** infrastructure as an example. This is a particularly relevant possible CC-04 alternative if it becomes an open-source *deployed and evaluated* tool. Research submissions are rolling monthly, with **mandatory previous-month 25th-day abstract enrollment**. Always check [submission guidelines](https://www.vldb.org/2027/submission-guidelines.html).
- **CIKM 2026 Applied Research:** explicitly invited academia and industry; use only as historical scope evidence until a 2027 call exists.
- **ICDE 2027 Industry and Applications, 1 Dec 2026 23:59 Pacific Time:** **must have non-academic coauthor**, even if university work is of production-level quality. Same venue's other research/vision tracks have separate calls and should not be confused with this restriction.
- **SIGMOD 2027 Industrial, 24 Nov 2026 AoE:** **must have non-academic coauthor**. An industry-sounding paper title is not a substitute.
- **ISCA 2027 Industry, abstract 6 Dec / paper 13 Dec AoE:** **first and most authors must work in industry**; student short-term internships specifically excluded. Software topics do not relax this author restriction.
- **CHIIR 2027 Industry Papers, 29 Oct 2026 AoE:** student internships are welcomed, but the proposed industrial-paper format asks for company/product context and company description. For a university-only paper, obtain chair clarification rather than assuming acceptance.
- **The Web Conference 2027 Industry:** closed 25 Oct 2026; requires demonstrated industrial-environment deployment/release duration. Its call does not clearly establish company-affiliation eligibility, so academic-only teams should ask organizers in a future cycle.

## What past accepted NAACL Industry papers actually look like

The [NAACL 2025 Industry proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/volumes/2025.naacl-industry/) are **peer-reviewed accepted-paper evidence**, not a prediction of 2027 acceptance. Representative contributions include:

- **Understanding LLM Development Through Longitudinal Study: Insights from the Open Ko-LLM Leaderboard** — studies **11 months** of public model-leaderboard evolution. Lesson: a careful applied evaluation of a real operational ecosystem can be a contribution; it does not automatically exempt a 2027 author from the current deployment-grounding CFP.
- **Finding-Centric Structuring of Japanese Radiology Reports and Analysis of Performance Gaps for Multiple Facilities** — evaluates cross-facility robustness with **8,428 clinical reports from seven facilities**. Lesson: real stakeholder data and documented domain-specific generalization failures are stronger than a generic new architecture claim.
- **RTSM: Knowledge Distillation with Diverse Signals for Efficient Real-Time Semantic Matching in E-Commerce** — addresses latency and quality of real-time semantic retrieval. Lesson: show measured operating constraints and deployment relevance, not merely accuracy.
- **How LLMs React to Industrial Spatio-Temporal Data? Assessing Hallucination with a Novel Traffic Incident Benchmark Dataset** — builds an application-grounded evaluation from real incident data and provides a public dataset. Lesson: useful industry evidence need not be private corporate data.

These are historical examples **under NAACL 2025's evaluation**; track topics, restrictions, policy and quality threshold must be rechecked for 2027. The official NAACL 2027 FAQ remains the authority for author eligibility.

## Matching existing CS Conf research branches to these tracks

- **CC-04 entity-resolution completion budgets:** the [VLDB 2027 Scalable Data Science](https://vldb.org/2027/call-for-research-track.html) research category is particularly relevant because its official CFP explicitly cites reusable entity-linkage infrastructure. A future **NAACL/COLING Industry** claim would require a genuine speech/text business application and actual practical evaluation; matched candidate-count toy experiments alone are not enough. **ICDE/SIGMOD industrial** would require an eligible non-academic coauthor even if CC-04 had a good systems contribution.
- **CC-08 research-agent verification:** NAACL Industry might fit only if an actual NLP-agent system is deployed/used beyond a controlled research setting and yields operationally valuable evidence. Existing prior-art overlap and lack of external deployment do not support immediate submission.
- **CC-02 adaptive masking and CC-05 pretraining:** general methodological work usually belongs in a research track; calling it industrial because it improves speed is insufficient without meaningful real-world application, lifecycle cost and operational constraints.
- **CC-09/CC-17 speech and audio experiments:** practical speech systems can fit some NLP Industry Tracks, but academic benchmark-only analysis and missing authorized evaluation datasets are not substitutes for field validation.
- **CC-13 network systems:** the IMC/SIGCOMM measurement/experience routes are different from Industry Track affiliation-gated product tracks; choose according to original evidence requirements, not conference prestige.

**Submissions are not ready by virtue of being eligible.** No new abstract, verified deployed system, independent evaluation or conference submission is claimed from this policy audit.

## Future hourly CS Conf rules

1. **Do not filter out an Industry Track just because the user is a research student.** Check every paper track against its actual CFP/FAQ and record **author eligibility independently from paper fit**.
2. Record year, track name, deadline (including AoE versus Pacific Time), whether abstract pre-registration is mandatory, page limit, peer review, proceedings status, company-coauthor requirement, AI-use restriction, submission overlap rule, and proof from the primary CFP.
3. Use **✓** only for explicit FAQ permission or genuinely open applied/research calls; do not infer "no company author needed" when the call only discusses industry use. Put **?** with a chair-query requirement.
4. Include applied and system-application categories alongside industry-labelled tracks, but clearly distinguish workshops, challenges, demonstrations, proposals and networking/industry-day talks from archival papers.
5. For each CS Conf research branch, match *requirements to evidence*: existing external users, observed operational constraints, realistic validated lab pilots, production-scale measurements, or a controlled new algorithm. Do not treat topic fit as paper readiness.
6. Re-audit year-specific CFPs whenever they appear. Do not copy 2026 eligibility into 2027 by assumption. Publish only public organizer evidence; private pilot measurements remain in the private GitHub issue.

**Coverage limitation:** This is a source-checked cross-domain survey, **not** a claim that every industry track worldwide has been audited. The university-only author question is unresolved when the official call does not answer it.
