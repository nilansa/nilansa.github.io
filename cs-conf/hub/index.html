04 / Provenance

Know what the archive can say.

Paper abstracts help find a direction. They do not prove that a method works or that a new combination is novel.

A

Source and attribution

Metadata comes from the CVPR_Papers dataset (MIT license). Paper and PDF links are matched to the Computer Vision Foundation Open Access listings. Six PDF links returned HTTP 404 to a HEAD check; details link to their year listing. Paper rights remain with their authors or other rights holders.

B

Track labels

A dataset label of “CVPR” is not enough to verify a main-track acceptance. The hub marks uncertain records as CVPR main candidate. A CVF URL or BibTeX field can confirm main or workshop status.

C

What “cluster” means

Clusters are simple keyword labels for discovery. They are not official subject areas. Two different labels do not prove that two methods are compatible or new.

D

What a hypothesis means

An “UNTESTED” card is a question to evaluate. It contains no model results or verified baseline. Accepted papers are useful examples, not a guarantee of future acceptance.
