04 / Provenance

Know what the archive can say.

Paper abstracts and source area labels help find a direction. They do not prove that a method works or that a new combination is novel.

A

Source and attribution

New venue/year metadata comes from the ai-conferences datasets. Existing CVPR files retain their earlier CVPR_Papers source and attribution. The catalog lists each revision, checksum, license declaration, and original publisher URL. Paper rights remain with their authors or other rights holders.

B

Track labels

These are curated venue records. A source track label is shown with its source attribution; it is not an independent check of main-track status. Missing track details remain unverified.

C

What a field label means

Field and topic labels come from source metadata or keyword fallbacks. They help discovery. They are not official rankings, acceptance predictions, or proof that two methods are compatible.

D

What a hypothesis means

An “UNTESTED” card is a question to evaluate. It contains no model results or verified baseline. Accepted papers are useful examples, not a guarantee of future acceptance.
