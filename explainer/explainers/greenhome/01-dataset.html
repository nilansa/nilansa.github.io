Source fields, dates, missing data and the crucial boundary between an observation and an assumption.

There are three different kinds of input

A · Real observed data

DESNZ NEED 2026

The anonymised public 50,000-property extract supplies building categories and valid annual 2024 gas/electricity observations.

B · User-provided facts

Property confirmation

Is the roof leaking? Is there a loft? Is the heating a gas boiler? None of these should be invented from NEED's broad fuel categories.

C · Scenario assumptions

Retrofit model inputs

Space-heating share, upgrade reduction percentages, heat-pump efficiency, solar generation, costs and tariffs.

Do not merge the three in your explanation. Observed energy comes from public meters; homeowner-entered conditions are scenario inputs; post-retrofit results are modelled counterfactuals, not observed before-and-after records.

Exactly which columns matter?

The source training code uses five categorical predictors and one annual target per fuel. The remaining fields are provenance, score inputs, annotations or filters—not extra inputs to the electricity/gas regressor.

What happens when a field is absent?

A missing fuel reading stays unavailable unless the owner deliberately requests a trained-model estimate. An unknown condition stays unknown and makes the retrofit conditional or unsupported. A neutral 50-point component is an explicit scoring convention, not evidence the house is average.

Four data-integrity rules judges may probe

1

Dates must line up

2024 energy targets are not silently joined with later works. The dataset builder retains later scheme dates but does not treat them as observed 2024 conditions.

2

Source rows are split once

Training makes one seeded 60/20/20 source-row partition. Valid fuel targets are selected within that partition, separately for electricity and gas. The anonymised data has one row per source property, but no address-level linkage to external tables.

3

No invented retrofit labels

NEED consumption observations are real. Retrofit outcomes are simulated; agreeing with your own simulator would not validate predictive accuracy on real homes.

4

Unknown is not zero

Missing consumption does not mean free energy; a missing loft scheme record does not prove bare rafters; a missing PV registration does not prove there are no panels.

A judge asks: “Why not simply join EPC and house-price records?”

Prepared answer: “The public anonymised NEED extract is deliberately not address-linked. We cannot safely match its rows to individual property sales. We therefore use it for energy modelling and separate published market associations for limited value sensitivity. We do not pretend this establishes a causal property-value uplift.”
