How does it work?

Three steps. One equation for each step. No manually drawn approximation.

01 / SAMPLE

Turn a border into points.

Follow the closed outline and resample it into 768 equal-distance locations. Each point has coordinates x and y. Treat x + iy as one complex-valued signal.

zⱼ = xⱼ + iyⱼ,  j = 0,…,767
02 / TRANSFORM

Discover the circles.

A discrete Fourier transform gives a coefficient cₙ for each frequency n. Its magnitude is the vector length, and its argument is the starting angle.

cₙ = (1/N) Σⱼ zⱼ e⁻ⁱⁿ²πʲ/ᴺ
03 / RECONSTRUCT

Rotate, then add.

A term with frequency n rotates through angle arg(cₙ) + nθ. Add all terms from −K to +K. Larger K follows the original curve more closely.

zₖ(θ) = c₀ + Σₙ₌₁…ₖ (cₙeⁱⁿᶿ + c₋ₙe⁻ⁱⁿᶿ)
