A stronger teacher is not enough.

Can a dense retriever learn more from a fixed budget of expensive cross-encoder judgments? We test the teacher, the student, and the supervision between them.

The SciFact full-gold setting has not shown a reliable incremental teacher gain. The final pre-registered test is C6: two domains, untouched official tests, and a 10%-gold regime.

01 / PIPELINE

Where does the teacher enter?

The target workflow. The selector decides which pairs consume the scoring budget.

06
EvaluationFull-corpus ranking on held-out queries
Student · bi-encoder

Two separate encodings

Encode the query and document independently. Compare their vectors. This is fast enough to search a large corpus.

Current student: all-MiniLM-L6-v2.

Teacher · cross-encoder

One joint query–document pass

Read the query and document together. The model can compare their words and context directly. This is stronger for pair scoring, but costly to run for many pairs.

Teachers tested: MiniLM cross-encoder, MedCPT, and BGE reranker.

Labels are not the same thing. Gold labels are benchmark relevance judgments (qrels). Teacher labels are model-generated scores for selected pairs. Distillation asks the student to learn from those scores; it does not turn them into human judgments.

Similar ANN recall can expose different training pairs.

A separate one-seed MS MARCO pilot motivates the candidate-selection stage. It did not use a teacher and is not evidence of a teacher-feedback gain.

What was actually measured?

SciFact is a scientific claim–evidence retrieval benchmark. It is not a purely biomedical corpus.

Why MedCPT? It was tested because it ranked the training candidates better than the cached MiniLM teacher. Its biomedical pretraining is a possible domain mismatch on SciFact, but that is not an established explanation for the weak student transfer.

What the results support

  • Gold relevance labels improve the SciFact student in the tested setup.
  • MedCPT is a stronger teacher-side reranker than the cached MiniLM teacher on TRAIN′.
  • Adding MedCPT KD to gold labels did not pass the C5 incremental-gain gate.

What they do not support

  • That teacher feedback improves the student retriever.
  • That the best teacher is necessarily the best student supervisor.
  • That any selection policy has beaten random or top-ranked selection.
  • That the result generalizes across retrieval domains.

06 / PRIOR WORK

Teacher supervision is not new. Budgeted acquisition is the question.

Closest precedents, separated by what they actually select or supervise.

PaperWhat it establishesBoundary for this project
TAS-Balanced · SIGIR 2021Dual-teacher signals and topic-aware, balanced-margin batch sampling.Sampling for training; not a fixed budget for fresh teacher calls.
RocketQA · NAACL 2021Cross-encoder denoising and pseudo-labeling of retrieved hard negatives.Teacher supervision; no budgeted acquisition policy.
