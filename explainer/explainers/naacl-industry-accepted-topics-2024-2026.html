4. ASDST100: From accepted precedent to your own credible paper

Read examples as patterns of demonstrated contribution, never as proof that copying the same idea will be publishable.

  1. Real user problem: state what breaks in the real workflow (not just “LLM accuracy is low”).
  2. Constraint: choose a measurable limit — p95 latency, memory, dollars per 1,000 tasks, hallucination harm, multilingual noise, drift or labor time.
  3. Simple baseline: use a practical baseline (BM25/RAG, an encoder model, a single agent, no-memory setup, one-call judge).
  4. Single testable contribution: change one component and specify why it should help.
  5. End-to-end evidence: test real workflows or faithful replay, not only isolated subtask scores. Publish denominators, hardware, variance and split methodology.
  6. Ablate and fail: compare quality against cost, include realistic failure slices, and say when the method is worse.
  7. Venue decision: show why findings are transferable to other practitioners, then check the 2027 requirements.

Three cheap, grounded research paths suggested by the precedents

Agent memory and browser automation

Relevant papers: ColorBrowserAgent, DIVERT, Enterprise Deep Research, UINav. Cheap test: realistic web tasks with controlled page changes; compare no memory, raw history, compact memory and verified memory; measure recovery rate, cost, end-to-end success and erroneous tool actions. Novelty warning: memory compression, browser agents and simulated users are established; a new failure regime or stronger causal evaluation is essential.

Small LMs, efficient pretraining and real deployment constraints

Relevant papers: Low-resource CPT, Tiny Titans, MeSP, Index-time prefixes. Cheap test: one narrow industrial task, CPU/consumer-GPU baselines and an explicit cost–quality curve; include language/domain shifts. Novelty warning: merely training a smaller LLM is not sufficient without application-specific findings.

Entity resolution, retrieval and noisy real-world records

Relevant papers: Structure-Guided Entity Resolution, GeoGround, Payment address parsing, REXEL. Cheap test: benchmark transliteration, missing fields, multilingual scripts, noisy addresses and tail cases; measure link-level recall and wrong-merge cost alongside inference latency. Novelty warning: cross-script and address-matching methods exist; demonstrate a specific uncovered failure and a replicable gain.

2027 Industry Track submission gate

Deadline: 31 October 2026, 23:59 AoE (1 November 2026, 17:29 IST). Paper: maximum 6 content pages; mandatory Limitations section; double-blind; direct OpenReview, not ARR. Abstract preregistration: no separate mandatory earlier abstract-registration deadline is listed in the official Industry CFP. Always recheck the organizer's page before submitting.
