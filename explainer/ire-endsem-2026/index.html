The question style

Four moves for most objective questions

01

Spot the symptom

p99 spikes, a stale read, a skewed shard, a stuffed document, or an apparent significant lift.

02

Name the principle

Little, CAP, MinHash, BM25, position bias, calibration or the meaning of p.

03

Choose and justify

Why this design or interpretation fits; why the tempting alternative violates an invariant.
