2026 MidSem / Part A

MinHash collision logic

Explain how similarity of shingle sets becomes a probability that signatures agree.

Open the worked model reasoning

A single MinHash coordinate agrees with probability Jaccard; the mean of k coordinate equalities estimates J with standard error about √(J(1−J)/k).

Signature size and estimator precision

Estimate number of hash coordinates and memory trade-off.

2026 MidSem / Part D

Two MapReduce jobs

Distinguish independent signature computation from band-key grouping.

Open the worked model reasoning

Job A per-doc signatures is map-only. Job B maps each (band index, hash-of-band) to docID, shuffles by that key, and reduces bucket members. B pays network shuffle; deterministic side-effect-free maps make retry safe.
