How to read source labels

Confirmed

Directly from the instructor's dated announcement or pre-reading; strongest guide to format and scope.

Provided primary

Topics and mechanics inside the uploaded lecture modules and assessment keys. We paraphrase rather than republish the full PDFs.

Extension

Useful general preparation when the A/B-testing syllabus is broad, but not a claim that the professor will ask it.
