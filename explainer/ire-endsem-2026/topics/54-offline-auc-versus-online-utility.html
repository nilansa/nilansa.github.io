Offline AUC versus online utility

An offline quality gain is a candidate for launch, not proof of live value.

High yieldProvided module reference

Basis: M3 p.39; M1 pp.16–19. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

AUC measures pairwise discriminative ranking, while conversion costs, latency and user behavior determine business impact. Training-serving skew, covariate shift and delayed feedback can break assumed relationships. Use guarded online experiments after offline validation.

Core principle
An offline quality gain is a candidate for launch, not proof of live value.

Try to reason through the mechanism

  1. Identify what the system is trying to preserve or measure in the situation.
  2. Separate the observed symptom from the cause described in this lesson.
  3. Choose the operation, model or trade-off that fits that cause; name its failure mode.
02 / Small example

A concrete case

An offline model wins AUC but a deployed feed loses cost per acquisition because online features differ.

Equation or decision rule

ROC-AUC = P(score_positive>score_negative)

Know what each symbol or action means. The instructor's announced final-exam format emphasizes applying principles to short objective scenarios.

03 / Examiner's trap

A tempting answer that fails

Equating a small AUC gain with a predictable increase in real revenue.

Use an explicit four-part answer: symptom → principle → mechanism → consequence. A strong answer explains why the apparently easy choice is unsafe or incomplete.
