Shingling and Jaccard

Shingles turn local token sequences into sets; Jaccard measures overlap relative to union.

CoreProvided module reference

Basis: M2 pp.10–11. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

Use word k-grams or character k-grams. Small k merges unrelated texts, large k is sensitive to small edits. Jaccard is intersection over union for sets, not simply overlap divided by the first document's size.

Core principle
Shingles turn local token sequences into sets; Jaccard measures overlap relative to union.

Try to reason through the mechanism

  1. Identify what the system is trying to preserve or measure in the situation.

Documents share four shingles and have seven distinct shingles total; Jaccard is 4/7≈0.57.
