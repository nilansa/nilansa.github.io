Variance reduction and CUPED

Pre-treatment information can reduce outcome noise without leaking treatment effects.

ExtensionSyllabus-derived extension

Basis: End-sem announcement 14 Nov; A/B testing extension. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

CUPED adjusts a measured outcome using a correlated baseline measured before assignment/exposure. Done correctly, it shrinks variance and improves precision. Using post-treatment variables can introduce bias rather than help.

Core principle
Pre-treatment information can reduce outcome noise without leaking treatment effects.

Try to reason through the mechanism

  1. Identify what the system is trying to preserve or measure in the situation.
  2. Separate the observed symptom from the cause described in this lesson.
  3. Choose the operation, model or trade-off that fits that cause; name its failure mode.
02 / Small example

A concrete case

Prior-week user activity predicts experimental-week activity; adjust with prior-week activity, not an action caused by the new UI.

Equation or decision rule

Y_adj = Y−θ(X_pre−mean X_pre)

A tempting answer that fails

Conditioning on a variable affected by the treatment.
