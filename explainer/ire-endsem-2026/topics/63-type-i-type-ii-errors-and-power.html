Lesson 63 / 69 · A/B testing

Type I, Type II errors and power

False-positive risk and missed-effect risk are distinct properties of a testing procedure.

High yieldSyllabus-derived extension

Basis: End-sem announcement 14 Nov; A/B testing extension. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

Type I is rejecting a true null at a long-run rate controlled by α under assumptions. Type II is failing to reject when a specified alternative is true; power is 1−β. Increasing sample size can improve power for a chosen effect while changing the testing procedure changes error behavior.

Core principle
False-positive risk and missed-effect risk are distinct properties of a testing procedure.

02 / Small example

A concrete case

A useful ranking lift is real but the sample is small and test misses it: a Type II error.

Equation or decision rule

power=1−β; type-I rate α
