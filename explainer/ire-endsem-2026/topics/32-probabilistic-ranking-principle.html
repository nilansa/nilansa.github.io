Probabilistic ranking principle

For binary relevance under a simple user utility model, rank by estimated probability of relevance.

CoreProvided module reference

Basis: M3 p.9. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

The PRP links ordering to decisions under uncertainty. In a basic model, rarer matches contribute log-odds evidence; with additional assumptions and no relevance labels, this resembles IDF. But the model is conditional on its loss and probability estimates.

Core principle
For binary relevance under a simple user utility model, rank by estimated probability of relevance.

02 / Small example

A concrete case

When two results have estimated relevance probabilities 0.8 and 0.2, showing the 0.8 item first maximizes expected early hits.

Equation or decision rule
