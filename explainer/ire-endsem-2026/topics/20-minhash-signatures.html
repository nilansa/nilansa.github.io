MinHash signatures

MinHash collision probability equals Jaccard similarity under suitable shared randomness.

CoreProvided module reference

Basis: M2 pp.12–14; MidSem key pp.1–2. Page labels point to the provided files or named reading; the explanation and exercises here are independently written.

01 / Understand

What actually happens?

For each hash/permutation, retain the minimum hashed shingle. The fraction of equal coordinates estimates Jaccard. More independent hash coordinates reduce estimator standard error as 1/√k, at greater signature storage and compute.

Core principle
MinHash collision probability equals Jaccard similarity under suitable shared randomness.

  1. Identify what the system is trying to preserve or measure in the situation.

02 / Small example

A concrete case

Two documents with J≈0.8 should share about 80 of 100 independent signature coordinates in expectation.

Equation or decision rule

Pr[h_min(A)=h_min(B)]=J(A,B); SE≈√(J(1−J)/k)

03 / Examiner's trap

A tempting answer that fails

Treating a single matching signature component as a proof of duplication.

Use an explicit four-part answer: symptom → principle → mechanism → consequence. A strong answer explains why the apparently easy choice is unsafe or incomplete.

04 / Active recall

Exam-style scenario

For J=0.8 and 100 independent MinHashes, how many coordinates agree on average?
One-line viva / short-answer question

Why does more k increase reliability?

Averaging more approximately independent Bernoulli collisions reduces uncertainty.
