How to interpret statuses

Done denotes an inspected source, verified artifact or proven configuration. Enabled is only proof the task is scheduled; it does not verify any run's outcome. Unverified denotes unavailable evidence, not failure. Historical public study pages are linked where they exist; publication of a study guide does not mean the assignment or experiment is complete.

Updating

The static pages change only with a separately reviewed publication. There is no automatic browser access to private files, schedules, computers or email. Other historical study pages remain indexed in the original ST100 catalog.
