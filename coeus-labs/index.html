01 / About us

Inquiry before acclaim.

Coeus Labs is an independent research initiative for studying how AI systems learn, retrieve information, remember, reason, and fail. Our aim is to separate a promising leaderboard result from a finding that generalizes. We value clear hypotheses, faithful baselines, negative results, and experiments others can reproduce.

02 / Research

What we study.

Four intersecting areas connect theory, systems, and empirical investigation. These are research interests, not claims of completed publications.

R / 01

Evaluation & reliability

Robust benchmarks, distribution shifts, controlled comparisons, and interpretable model behaviour across AI systems.

R / 02

Efficient learning

Training-data selection, curriculum design, and lower-cost strategies for learning under constrained resources.

R / 03

Retrieval & memory

Information retrieval, persistent knowledge, memory policies, and long-horizon evaluation of intelligent agents.

R / 04

Multimodal intelligence

Video understanding, temporal reasoning, consistency, and grounded multimodal model evaluation.

04 / How we publish

Evidence first.
Publications second.

Challenges and hackathons are useful testing grounds, but a leaderboard position is not itself a research contribution. Our publication standard prioritizes original findings and reproducibility.

01
Make the comparison fairDocument data splits, baselines, compute budgets, and uncertainty.
02
Keep a transparent research recordSeparate exploratory notes and competition reports from peer-reviewed research.
03
Credit every contributorRepresent contributions and academic affiliations accurately.
04
Release what can be verifiedShare code, methods, and evaluation details whenever permitted.
