Research-year strategy

Nov–Jan · Apply

Build one strong academic CV, transcripts, a research portfolio and referee list. Tailor motivation to each lab; prioritize the earliest hard cutoffs.

Feb–Apr · Interview & secure

Prepare research discussions, track decisions, confirm paid terms and handle institute onboarding, visa and travel documentation.

May–Aug · Do research

Target a defined research problem, clear baselines, reproducible experiments, mentor feedback and—when realistic—a paper or project report.

Sep–Oct · Convert experience

Publish permitted work, collect references and identify the following cycle of research internships, RA positions and fully funded training pathways.

Financial eligibility: Paid does not always mean fully financed. Some positions offer a salary or stipend, others provide room and travel reimbursement; taxes, accommodation, visa rules and outside funding restrictions differ. All program dates, pay, and openings should be confirmed on the organizer site before an application decision.
