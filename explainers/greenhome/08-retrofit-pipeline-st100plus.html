Physical vs computational retrofit: GreenHome simulates the upgrades and recommends their order. An actual home retrofit requires surveys, permissions where applicable, an installer-designed specification, installation/commissioning and later meter-data validation. Those real-world steps are not automatically performed by this app.

What installers actually do in the real house

The app's five measures correspond to real building work. The physical steps below are general building-practice context, not inspections GreenHome has performed.

1 · Roof / loft

Loft insulation

Inspect for roof leaks and ventilation, check whether accessible insulation is already present, choose appropriate material/thickness, install without obstructing ventilation, and verify the work.

In code: a roof leak or absent loft blocks this measure.

2 · Windows

Double glazing

Survey the existing windows and planning constraints; assess thermal suitability and ventilation; replace eligible glazing/frames with properly fitted sealed double-glazed units.

In code: confirmed double/triple glazing or restricted planning blocks this proposed replacement.

3 · Heating control

Smart controls

Check compatibility with the heating system, install thermostat/programmer or appropriate controls, set schedules and commission/test their response.

In code: no saving is assigned for unsupported systems.

4 · Heating conversion

Air-source heat pump

Carry out a heat-loss survey, check emitters and hot-water system, siting and electrical capacity, install outdoor/indoor equipment, then commission and optimise temperatures.

In code: models a gas-boiler replacement, with COP 3 and boiler efficiency 85% assumptions.

5 · Electricity generation

Rooftop solar PV

Check roof condition, structural loading, shading, permissions and grid connection; install modules, inverter and protective equipment, then test and commission.

In code: solar offsets imported electricity, not electricity demand.

6 · Verify results

Measure after completion

Compare weather- and occupancy-aware post-installation bills/meter readings with baseline. This validation is a future production requirement, not supplied by the current anonymised dataset.

In code: no causal retrofit outcome model is trained.

Example question: “If I add a heat pump, why might my electricity bill increase?”

Correct explanation: the simulator replaces estimated gas space-heating demand with electric heating demand using the assumed boiler efficiency and heat-pump COP. It therefore may show a positive gas saving but negative electricity saving. The answer should show the two fuels separately.

Not Gemma; not a free-form numeric model. The frozen submission selects qwen3:4b locally, with deepseek-r1:7b as a fallback. It uses JSON schemas, restricted fact IDs, confirmation gates and deterministic explanations. No Gemma integration or LLM fine-tuning is present. An optional Qwen3-VL image pathway was configured but not verified as installed in the final run.
